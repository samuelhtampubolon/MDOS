From a business question to defensible marketing evidence

Research, strategy scenarios and a customer journey you can test, in one app. Agents do the legwork, numbers always come from code, and people approve every decision.

Download the desktop app View the source

Windows, macOS and Linux. The Lake Toba demo uses synthetic data.

Three modules, one closed loop

Research Lab

Turns a question into a research design and a bilingual questionnaire, cleans the data, runs 15 statistical methods with assumption checks and drafts insights.

Strategy Simulator

Builds a market model from the evidence and tests price, budget and audience changes with sensitivity and Monte Carlo risk.

Journey Designer

Maps the customer journey, finds friction in reviews and turns the best fixes into A/B tests whose results return as evidence.

Research that stays defensible

  • Every analysis shows its sample size, assumption checks and limitations.
  • Insights must cite evidence, traceable back to the data version.
  • Causal words are blocked unless the evidence comes from an experiment.
  • Only a person can approve a verdict, a decision or a cleaning step.
